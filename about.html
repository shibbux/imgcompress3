About Image Compressor

Image Compressor with Bulk Download is a free, open-source, privacy-first image optimization tool designed to help you compress, resize, and edit photos instantly—completely in your browser, with no uploads to external servers.

Our Mission

We believe image optimization should be:

  • Private: Your images never leave your device
  • Fast: Processing happens instantly in your browser
  • Easy: Simple, intuitive interface for all users
  • Free: No signup, no hidden costs, no ads
  • Powerful: Advanced features for power users

Developer

Image Compressor is developed and maintained by Shivam. For inquiries or support, reach out at shahshivam732@gmail.com.

Technology Stack

Image Compressor is built entirely with client-side technologies:

  • HTML5: Semantic markup for accessibility
  • CSS3: Modern responsive design with Tailwind CSS
  • JavaScript (ES6+): Canvas API for image processing
  • JSZip: Bulk download functionality

No server-side processing means maximum privacy and speed.

Key Features

  • ✅ Compress Images: Reduce file size while maintaining quality
  • ✅ Resize Photos: Custom width, height, and aspect ratios
  • ✅ Apply Filters: Grayscale, sepia, blur, invert, vintage effects
  • ✅ View EXIF Data: See and manage image metadata
  • ✅ Bulk Download: Process and download multiple images at once
  • ✅ Multiple Formats: Support for JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF
  • ✅ No Signup Required: Use immediately, no account needed
  • ✅ Works Offline: After initial load, works without internet

Privacy Commitment

Your privacy is our priority:

  • 🔒 Zero Server Uploads: Images processed entirely in your browser
  • 🔒 No Data Collection: We don't track or store your images
  • 🔒 No Accounts Required: Anonymous usage, no personal info needed
  • 🔒 Open Source: Code is transparent and auditable

Read our Privacy Policy for complete details.

Why Image Compressor?

vs. Online Tools: Most online compressors upload your images to their servers. Image Compressor keeps everything on your device.

vs. Desktop Software: No installation needed. Works on any device with a modern browser—PC, Mac, Linux, phone, or tablet.

vs. Native Apps: No app store, no permissions, no storage space required. Just open in your browser.
